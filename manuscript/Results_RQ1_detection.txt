In every one of the 60 pairs, the watermarked translation scored higher on both measures (Figure 1). With the correct key, the mean rose from 0.5003 to 0.5403 (mean paired increase 0.0399, cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.0372, 0.0428]). Across the five incorrect key sets, the mean barely changed, from 0.5002 to 0.4999. The gap widened by 0.0402 [0.0372, 0.0434], pointing to a signal specific to the correct key.

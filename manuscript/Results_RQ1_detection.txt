The clearest change appeared when the detector used the correct key. The mean score was 0.5003 without watermarking and 0.5403 with it, a paired increase of 0.0399 (cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.0372, 0.0428]). The mean across five incorrect key sets changed little, from 0.5002 to 0.4999. Watermarking therefore widened the gap by 0.0402 [0.0372, 0.0434]. Figure 1 shows that the watermarked translation had both the higher correct-key score and the wider gap in all 60 pairs.

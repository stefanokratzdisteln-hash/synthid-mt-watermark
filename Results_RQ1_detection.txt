RQ1: Detection outcome

Primary outcome: correct-key mean g-score. Enabling SynthID-Text raised the correct-key
mean g-score from 0.5003 (SD = 0.0059; range 0.4878-0.5144) in the watermark-disabled
condition to 0.5403 (SD = 0.0085; range 0.5246-0.5755) in the watermark-enabled condition.
The paired mean difference was +0.0399 (95% CI [0.0372, 0.0427]), t(59) = 28.97, p < .001,
Cohen's d_z = 3.74, and all 60 pairs moved in the same direction. The two conditions did not
overlap at the level of individual translations: the highest score in the disabled condition
(0.5144) remained below the lowest score in the enabled condition (0.5246), so every
translation was assigned to its own condition by this score alone. The disabled condition
also sat at 0.5003, statistically indistinguishable from the 0.5 expectation for an
unwatermarked sequence, which indicates that the scoring procedure itself introduces no
baseline inflation.

Secondary outcome: score gap. The score gap, defined as the correct-key mean g-score minus
the average mean g-score obtained with five incorrect keys, was 0.00014 (SD = 0.00716;
range -0.0166 to 0.0158) without the watermark and 0.04035 (SD = 0.00929; range 0.0208 to
0.0749) with it, a paired mean difference of +0.0402 (95% CI [0.0372, 0.0433]),
t(59) = 26.41, p < .001, Cohen's d_z = 3.41. Here too all 60 pairs increased, and the two
conditions did not overlap (disabled maximum 0.0158 below enabled minimum 0.0208).

The score gap thus rose by essentially the same amount as the correct-key mean g-score
itself, and because the gap in the disabled condition was indistinguishable from zero, the
increase under SynthID-Text is attributable to the correct key matching the generation-time
key rather than to a general elevation of g-scores; the secondary outcome therefore
corroborates the primary one.
